Help and support

Last updated November 19, 2026. Large Print Reader is made by BigPoppaDev.

Email for help

Write to lazyfreelancer1@gmail.com. It helps to say:

  • Which website or page you were reading.
  • What you clicked, and what happened.
  • Whether you have Plus.

Opening large print

Open a news story or blog post, then click the Large Print Reader button in Chrome’s toolbar. You can also press Alt + Shift + R.

Don’t see the button? Click the puzzle-piece button at the top right of Chrome, then click the pin next to Large Print Reader.

Going back to the page

Click the button again, press Esc, or choose Close. The page returns exactly as it was.

When a page won’t open in large print

Large Print Reader needs a page with a main block of text. On a homepage or a list of headlines, use Chrome’s own zoom instead: hold Ctrl and press +. On a Mac, hold Command and press +.

Chrome also blocks extensions on its own pages, the Chrome Web Store and PDFs.

Changing the size and colors

Use the big buttons at the top of the reading view: Smaller, Bigger, Colors, Spacing and Letters. The plus and minus keys change the size too.

Your choices are saved, so the next article opens the same way. More choices, like line length and pictures, are on the settings page. To open it, right-click the Large Print Reader button and choose Options.

Read aloud

Read aloud is part of Large Print Reader Plus. In the reading view, press Read aloud, then Get Plus.

Already paid on another computer? Press Read aloud, then I already paid, and use the email address you paid with.

No sound? Check that your computer’s sound is on and turned up. Read aloud uses the speech voices built into your browser.

Cancelling and refunds

Cancel any time on the settings page: choose Manage or cancel Plus. Email within 14 days of a charge and that charge is refunded. Read how refunds and cancelling work.

Privacy

Large Print Reader doesn’t collect what you read. The privacy page lists what it saves in your browser and why.
