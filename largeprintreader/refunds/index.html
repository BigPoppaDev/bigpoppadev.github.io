Refunds and cancelling

Last updated November 18, 2026. Large Print Reader is made by BigPoppaDev.

What this covers

The large print view is free. This page is about Large Print Reader Plus, the upgrade that reads articles out loud.

Plus will cost $2 a month. It isn’t on sale yet, so nobody has been charged.

Cancelling

You can cancel any time. Open the extension’s settings page and choose Manage or cancel Plus.

Or email lazyfreelancer1@gmail.com from the address you paid with and say you want to cancel.

You keep Plus until the end of the month you already paid for, and you aren’t charged again.

Refunds

Email within 14 days of a charge and that charge is refunded. The money goes back to the card you paid with. Your bank may take several days to show it.

How the charge looks on your statement

The charge appears as LARGE PRINT READER.

A charge you don’t recognize

Email lazyfreelancer1@gmail.com with the date and the amount.
