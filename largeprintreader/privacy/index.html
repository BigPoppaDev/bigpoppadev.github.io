Privacy

Last updated November 18, 2026. This page covers the Large Print Reader extension (version 0.2.0) and this website. Large Print Reader is made by BigPoppaDev.

What the extension collects about your reading

Nothing. Large Print Reader works on the page you choose, only when you click its button or press its shortcut. It doesn’t collect or send the pages you open, the articles you read, or your browsing history. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Pictures in an article load from the website you are reading, just as they would on the original page.

What stays in your browser

Your choices are saved with Chrome’s own storage so they are there next time: text size, colors, space between lines, letter style, line length, whether pictures show, and reading speed.

The extension also saves a few things for Plus:

  • Whether you have Plus, so read aloud keeps working if your internet drops.
  • For the payment system, ExtensionPay: the date you installed the extension and, once you choose Get Plus or I already paid, an account key. If you buy Plus, your email address and whether your subscription is active are saved too.

If you have Chrome sync turned on, Chrome may copy these to your other computers through your Google account. BigPoppaDev never receives them. Removing the extension removes what it saved in your browser.

What Chrome lets the extension do

  • Work on the tab you are looking at, after you click the button or press the shortcut.
  • Save your settings.

It has no access to your other tabs, or to pages you haven’t opened it on.

Read aloud

Read aloud is part of Plus. It uses the speech voices your browser offers, and it picks a voice installed on your computer when one fits the article’s language.

If the only matching voice is an online one, your browser sends the text to that voice’s provider to be spoken. With Chrome’s Google voices, that provider is Google. BigPoppaDev doesn’t receive the text either way.

Paying for Plus

Plus isn’t on sale yet. When it is, payment will be handled by two companies, ExtensionPay and Stripe.

  • You give an email address when you pay.
  • Your card details go to Stripe. BigPoppaDev never sees your full card number.
  • ExtensionPay keeps your email and whether your subscription is active. The extension checks with ExtensionPay to see if Plus should be turned on.
  • BigPoppaDev can see your email, what you paid and when, so it can answer questions and give refunds.

The extension doesn’t contact ExtensionPay or Stripe at all until you press Get Plus or I already paid. After that, it checks your plan with ExtensionPay when you press Read aloud or open the settings page. That check sends only the account key, never what you are reading.

ExtensionPay and Stripe each have their own privacy policy, on their own websites.

If you email support

Your email address and your message arrive in the support inbox. They are used to answer you.

This website

This site doesn’t ask you for anything and shows no ads. It is hosted by an outside company, which may keep ordinary server logs.

Selling or sharing

BigPoppaDev doesn’t sell or rent personal information, and doesn’t use it for advertising or to decide credit or lending. It is shared only with the payment companies named above, to take payments, or when the law requires it.

The use of information received through the extension follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Children

Large Print Reader isn’t meant for children under 13, and BigPoppaDev doesn’t knowingly collect information from them. Buying Plus is for adults.

Your choices

  • Change or reset your reading settings at any time on the extension’s settings page.
  • Cancel Plus at any time. See Refunds and cancelling.
  • Remove the extension, which deletes what it saved in your browser.
  • Ask what information BigPoppaDev holds about you, or ask for it to be deleted, by email. Some payment records have to be kept for tax and legal reasons.

Changes to this page

When this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. If a change affects what is collected, the extension’s store listing will say so too.
