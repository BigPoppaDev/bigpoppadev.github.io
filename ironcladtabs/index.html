Save your tabs. Never lose them.

Ironclad Tabs closes every tab in a window with one click and keeps them in a list you can open again anytime. It's built so your saved tabs survive updates, mistakes and bad imports.

Questions? Email us

What it does

One-click save

Every tab in the window is saved as a group and closed. Pinned tabs stay put.

Checked before closing

Each save is read back and checked. If anything doesn't match, no tab is closed.

Daily backups

A copy of all your saved tabs is made every day and kept for 3 days.

Undo

Deleted a group or a tab by mistake? One click puts it back exactly.

Export and import

Keep a backup file anywhere you like, and bring it back on any computer.

Coming from OneTab?

Paste OneTab's export list and your tabs come right over.

Private by design

No account and no sign-up. Your saved tabs stay in Chrome on your computer. Ironclad Tabs has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sends your tabs anywhere. Read the privacy policy.
