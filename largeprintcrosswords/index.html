What’s in it

Big letters from the start
The grid and the clues open in large print. Text size and contrast settings let you go further.
New puzzles any time
Three free themes, each with a new puzzle whenever you want one.
Your progress, saved
Puzzles you’re working on, puzzles you’ve solved and your daily streak are saved as you go.
More themes
Extra theme packs are one-time purchases.
Sign-in, only if you want it
Signing in with Google will be optional. It lets your progress follow you to your other devices.

Free and paid

Free

Three themes and new puzzles any time. The free version shows ads.

Paid extras

Theme packs add new puzzle themes. Remove ads turns off every ad in the app.

Each is a one-time purchase through Google Play, which handles the payment and any refunds.

What the app keeps

Your puzzles, streak and settings are saved on your device. If you sign in with Google, a copy is saved to your account so it can follow you to your other devices. We don’t sell your information.

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect information about your device to show them.
