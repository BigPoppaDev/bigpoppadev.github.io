Privacy

Effective date: November 19, 2026. Large Print Crosswords (“the app”) is made by BigPoppaDev. This page explains what information the app uses, and why.

The short version

  • Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Google, the app receives your name, email address and profile picture, and uses them only to keep your progress with your account.
  • Your puzzles, progress, streaks and settings are saved on your device. If you sign in, a copy is also saved to our online database so it can follow you to your other devices.
  • If you sign in, you can ask us to delete your account, and everything saved with it, at any time.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect information about your device to show and measure those ads.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card or payment details.

Information kept on your device

The app saves your puzzles in progress, the puzzles you have solved, your daily streaks, your text size and contrast settings, the themes you have unlocked, and whether you have removed ads. If you don’t sign in, this stays on your device and is not sent to us, and deleting the app deletes it.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. The app works without it. Signing in only lets your progress follow you to another device.

If you choose to sign in, Google shares your name, email address, profile picture and an account ID with the app. We use them only to:

  • sign you in and keep you signed in
  • save your progress, streaks, settings and the themes you have unlocked to your account, so they can follow you to your other devices

This information is stored in our online database, which a hosting provider runs for us. We don’t sell it, use it for ads, or share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it. We never see your Google password.

Large Print Crosswords’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove the app’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Ads from Google AdMob

The free version of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often they appear, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, Google’s software in the app may collect and use:

  • your device’s advertising ID
  • your IP address, which can show your approximate location
  • details about your device and the app, such as the phone model, operating system and app version
  • how you interact with the ads

Google may use this information to show ads based on your interests, where the law allows it. In places that require it, such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalized ads, in your phone’s settings. Buying Remove ads stops all ads in the app.

You can read how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Remove ads and extra theme packs are one-time purchases made through Google Play or the Apple App Store. They take your payment under their own privacy policies. The app only receives a confirmation of what was bought, so it can unlock it. We do not receive your name or payment details.

App updates

The app checks Expo’s update service for fixes and new puzzles. That check sends basic technical details, such as the app version and the type of device, and no personal information.

Children

The app is made for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. If you sign in, your account information and saved progress are stored by our database hosting provider, only so the app can work. Information that Google collects through the ads is handled under Google’s own privacy policy.

Deleting your account and information

If you never sign in, your information stays on your device, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

If you signed in, you can ask us to delete your account at any time. Email lazyfreelancer1@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my Large Print Crosswords account.” We will delete your account and everything saved with it, and reply to let you know when it’s done.

For ad information, use your phone’s ad settings or Google’s privacy controls, or contact us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
