Privacy

Effective date: November 18, 2026. Large Print Crosswords (“the app”) is made by BigPoppaDev. This page explains what information the app uses, and why.

The short version

  • There is no account or sign-in. The app never asks for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your puzzles, progress, streaks and settings stay on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect information about your device to show and measure those ads.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card or payment details.

Information kept on your device

The app saves your puzzles in progress, the puzzles you have solved, your daily streaks, your text size and contrast settings, the themes you have unlocked, and whether you have removed ads. This stays on your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Ads from Google AdMob

The free version of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often they appear, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, Google’s software in the app may collect and use:

  • your device’s advertising ID
  • your IP address, which can show your approximate location
  • details about your device and the app, such as the phone model, operating system and app version
  • how you interact with the ads

Google may use this information to show ads based on your interests, where the law allows it. In places that require it, such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalized ads, in your phone’s settings. Buying Remove ads stops all ads in the app.

You can read how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Remove ads and extra theme packs are one-time purchases made through Google Play or the Apple App Store. They take your payment under their own privacy policies. The app only receives a confirmation of what was bought, so it can unlock it. We do not receive your name or payment details.

App updates

The app checks Expo’s update service for fixes and new puzzles. That check sends basic technical details, such as the app version and the type of device, and no personal information.

Children

The app is made for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing and selling

We do not collect your personal information directly, and we do not sell it. Information that Google collects through the ads is handled under Google’s own privacy policy.

Deleting your information

The app has no accounts and keeps your information on your device, so uninstalling the app deletes it. For ad information, use your phone’s ad settings or Google’s privacy controls, or contact us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
